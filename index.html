1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in to Wicketly, we collect your email address to authenticate your account, provide access to the app, and associate your activity with your account.

We may use your email address to send account-related communications, such as login verification, security notifications, or important service updates, where applicable.

Match and Cricket Information

When you create or manage cricket matches, we may collect and store information such as:

  • Match details, including team names, match dates, venues, and match formats.
  • Cricket scores, overs, wickets, innings, and ball-by-ball scoring information.
  • Player names and other player details that you provide.
  • Team information, match statistics, and related records.
  • Match livestream links or other match-related information you submit, if supported by the app.

Please ensure you have appropriate permission to submit other people's personal information.

Technical Information

Our service providers or technical infrastructure may process technical information necessary to operate the app, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability. This may include IP addresses, device or app information, and diagnostic logs, depending on the services and technologies used.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Allow users to create, manage, and score cricket matches.
  • Maintain match records, team information, player details, and statistics.
  • Provide match-related features, including livestream links where available.
  • Protect accounts and help prevent misuse.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.

3. Storage and Retention

Account information and match-related information may be stored on our servers or through service providers used to operate Wicketly.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain match records, meet applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our services.

Deleting the app from your device does not necessarily delete your account or information stored on our servers. To request account or data deletion, contact us using the email address listed in the Contact Us section. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and retention obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: With providers that support hosting, authentication, database storage, security, or other essential app functionality.
  • Public match information: Match scores, team names, player names, statistics, or other match details may be visible to other users if you use features that publish or share match information.
  • External platforms: If you choose to share a livestream link or other information through an external service, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Legal and security requirements: Where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of our services.

We do not control the privacy practices of external platforms.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Wicketly is intended for people involved in organising, scoring, or following cricket matches. Users should provide only information they are authorised to share.

If the app is used by children or includes children's player information, applicable child privacy laws and relevant parental consent requirements must be followed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
